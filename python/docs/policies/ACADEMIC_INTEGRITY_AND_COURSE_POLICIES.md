# DATA120 — Academic Integrity and Course Policies

> **Source:** Course policy text provided by the student on 2026-11-04. This document is a working summary and checklist, **not a replacement** for the course outline, individual assignment instructions, or instructor rulings. The course policy is controlling if this summary differs.

## 1. Academic integrity

University of Calgary expects learners to follow its academic integrity and honesty policies.

**DATA120 action:** Keep research decisions, data interpretation, model selection, evaluation, and final conclusions your own. Preserve a record of your work and attribution.

Official course link: https://conted.ucalgary.ca/info/integrity.jsp

## 2. Citations and references

The provided course text recommends **APA** style when citing others' work **or your own prior work**.

**DATA120 action:** Document sources of code, data, algorithms, statistical definitions, images, figures, and previous work as appropriate. Use in-text citations and a reference list in the report and presentation, following assignment instructions.

Links supplied in the syllabus:
- https://www.ucalgary.ca/cted/breeze/referencing (legacy course link; verify availability)
- https://owl.english.purdue.edu/owl/resource/560/01/ (legacy Purdue link)

Current Purdue OWL APA guide: https://owl.purdue.edu/owl/research_and_citation/apa_style/apa_style_introduction.html

## 3. Student use of generative AI (GAI)

Course-provided requirements:
- Use GAI **only where each assignment or learning activity permits it**.
- AI may **support your own thoughts**, not replace individual work and analysis or create assessment answers in place of your own work.
- Do not upload peers' work into AI tools to generate peer feedback **without explicit consent**.
- **Reference and cite** any permitted GAI use following course instructions.
- Check accuracy, bias, security, and privacy risks in AI output.
- GAI use outside allowed guidelines or without proper citation **can constitute academic misconduct**.
- When uncertain, ask the instructor before proceeding.

Course-provided GAI citation guidance: https://conted.ucalgary.ca/info/writing.jsp

**DATA120 action:** Before asking GAI to write code, interpret results, draft assignment content, or evaluate a model, verify that use is permitted for that particular assignment. Record assistance in [AI_USE_LOG.md](AI_USE_LOG.md). A log helps transparency but **does not replace the required formal citation**.

The course text also states that the instructor will not upload student work to GAI without the student's explicit consent.

## 4. Copyright

Students and instructors must comply with Canada's Copyright Act and UCalgary fair-dealing guidance when distributing or posting materials.

**DATA120 action:** Do not upload lecture PDFs, textbook chapters, purchased datasets, third-party images, or other protected items to this public GitHub repository without permission or an applicable legal basis. Use citations and appropriate licensing; attribution alone does not confer redistribution rights.

UCalgary Copyright Office: https://library.ucalgary.ca/copyright
Canadian Copyright Act: https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/C-42/

## 5. Privacy and confidentiality

The supplied course text says that submitted assignments remain confidential with the instructor unless stated otherwise before submission, and that a learner's personal information will not be shared without permission. It cites the Freedom of Information and Protection of Privacy (FOIP) Act.

**Important distinction:** Those course assurances do **not** make a public GitHub repository, Google Sheet, or third-party AI service confidential. Never place identifiable parking records, real licence plates, pseudonymization keys, or sensitive clinic/business information in this public repository or send them to an AI tool without appropriate authorization and safeguards.

**Legal currency:** The course wording is reproduced in substance as supplied; privacy laws and institutional policies may change. Confirm current Alberta and UCalgary rules rather than assuming the course's FOIP wording is a complete, current legal analysis.
